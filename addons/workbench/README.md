# Workbench

Blender's Workbench shading for Godot's 3D editor viewport: a **Workbench** toggle in the viewport toolbar (Alt+Z) lights every surface with a fixed studio light or a matcap, in flat or texture colour, with cavity, outlines, crease lines and x-ray, and leaves the scene's own lights, fog, glow, ambient and tonemapping out of it. Nothing in the scene is touched.

Enable it under Project > Project Settings > Plugins. Options are in the menu beside the toggle and under Editor Settings > Workbench. Godot 4.7 or later, Forward+ only.

Full README, screenshots and changelog: https://github.com/reidhannaford/godot-workbench
